# Widows Hope

Website Development Prompt: Olori Adeola Relief Foundation

The goal of this project is to create a professional, inspiring, and transparent website that encourages donations and volunteerism for the foundation's 'Widows Food Bank' program.

1. Design & Branding Requirements

Aesthetic: Clean, modern, community-oriented, and uplifting. Avoid a "heavy" or sad tone. The primary focus should be on empowerment and community action.

Color Palette (Inspired by image): White (cleanliness/purity), Deep Green (growth/hope/relief), and warm Accents (Gold/Yellow/Red) for calls-to-action.

Typography: A bold, warm serif font for titles (like the one used for "WIDOWS FOOD BANK") and a clean, highly readable sans-serif for body text and navigation.

Key Design Elements: Incorporate stylized patterns (perhaps Nigerian textile-inspired) as subtle background overlays in section transitions, similar to the pattern in the source graphic. Use high-quality photos (real beneficiaries/volunteers).

2. Website Structure & Content

I. Site Header & Navigation

Left: A professionally redesigned logo (referencing the original: colorful interlocking rings/figures with text "Olori Adeola Relief Foundation").

Right (Navigation): Home, About Us, Our Programs, Gallery, Donate Now (Call to Action Button).

Tagline: "Breaking Barriers, Building Futures."

II. Hero Section (Home Page Top)

Background: An eye-catching collage or slideshow featuring happy beneficiaries, the organized "Widows Food Bank" items, and the foundation's team in action. Use the stylized image frame from the original graphic as a visual container for one of the key photos.

Main Title (Gold/Serif): "A Widow, but NOT Wasted."

Subtitle (White/Sans-Serif): "Olori Adeola Relief Foundation is dedicated to empowering and uplifting widows through essential food support and sustainable community programs."

Call to Action: A primary button, "Support Our Mission."

III. Mission & Impact Section

Section Title: "Why We Exist" or "Our Mission."

Content: Expand on "A widow but not wasted." Focus on breaking barriers (social stigma, economic hardship) and building futures. Use icons for "Food Security," "Community Empowerment," and "Dignity Support."

IV. Widows Food Bank Program (Detailed Section)

Section Title: "Widows Food Bank: Providing Relief and Hope."

Concept: Explain the program clearly. The objective is to ensure no widow goes hungry.

Visual Gallery (Use image references): A grid displaying the types of support provided:

Image 1: A stack of diverse food staples (e.g., Golden Penny Semovita, Nestlé Milo, BIG BULL rice bags).

Image 2: Beneficiaries smiling, holding their food packages in organized distributions (like the people featured in the original image).

Tagline: "Feed the Widow Program 2025" (Highlight this as an ongoing initiative).

V. Call to Action / Donate Section (The 'How to Help')

Section Title: "How You Can Be Part of the Solution."

Content: Create two distinct pathways for support.

Column A: Financial Support (Make this prominent)

Heading: "Make a Donation."

Instructions: "Your financial contributions allow us to purchase essential food staples in bulk and support other foundation initiatives."

Details (Formatted clearly in a box):

Bank Name: Wema Bank

Account Number: 0126951057

Account Name: Olori Adeola Relief Foundation

Visual: An icon of a secure donation shield.

Column B: Food Stuff Support (Direct Giving)

Heading: "Donate Food Items."

Instructions: "Coordinate direct food donations (Rice, Grains, Semovita, etc.) by contacting our team via WhatsApp."

Details (Formatted clearly in a box):

WhatsApp Contact (Only): 08184344442

(Optional inclusion of small food icons next to text)

VI. Testimonials / Gallery (Social Proof)

Title: "Your Impact in Their Words."

Content: Simple, moving quotes (or video snippets if available) from widows who have benefited from the program. Use portrait-style photos.

VII. Footer

Content:

Center: Redesigned Logo.

Address/Contact: Main physical address (if available) and an email address.

Tagline: "Breaking Barriers, Building Futures."

Final Text: "Thanks for your generosity!! All donations are vital in continuing this work."

Social Icons: (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, etc.).

3. Development Notes (For the Developer)

Responsibility: The site must be fully responsive and optimized for viewing on mobile phones, as many donors will be accessing information that way.

Optimization: Use compressed images to ensure fast load times without sacrificing visual quality.

Call to Action Placements: Ensure clear "Donate" or "How to Help" buttons are visible from every page (especially in the header).
